              Fair use

              OpenStreetMap and CARTO tile servers are donated infrastructure and their usage policies forbid bulk downloading. This tool caps a single download at 6 000 tiles and throttles requests. Grab the towns you actually travel, not whole states. For serious offline coverage, self-host tiles or buy a commercial plan.
